PRESERVE AND SHARE RESULTS
==========================

The result-preparation command prints the exact archive inventory and SHA-256
checksum. Review both before sharing. Raw arguments, paths, process output, and
error text can contain identifying information. The tool inventories files; it
does not promise that arbitrary captured content is private.

Preferred route
---------------

1. Copy the .tar.gz archive and its .sha256 file to another USB drive or a
   mounted persistent data volume.
2. Reread and verify the checksum on a stable machine.
3. Upload from that stable machine and share a view-only link.

Browser fallback
----------------

Only after every induced-load worker has stopped, you may use Firefox included
with the Ubuntu live image to upload the archive to Google Drive, Dropbox, or
another provider. A small archive can instead be attached in Gmail or another
mail provider. Prefer storage plus a view-only link for a larger archive.

Close Firefox during the measured run. Avoid signing into an important account
on a machine you consider unstable when removable media is available. Log out
when finished, then shut down the live session. Browser data and credentials
are not part of the evidence bundle and must never be collected by this kit.

Nothing in this kit uploads automatically or asks for cloud credentials.

Storage note
------------

The prepared archive publisher supports ordinary FAT/exFAT removable media.
The active raw result directory has stricter Unix permission and locking
requirements; see README.txt and the public live-kit guide.
